About the game

Most golf games end when the match ends. In Tee Wars, the hole stays yours. Beat the score that holds it and your flag goes up. Your turf pays coins every hour you hold it. Shield the holes you care about. When someone takes one, PAYBACK it. Own all nine and wear the crown.

Every rival is a real player's recorded round, so the score you have to beat was really shot on that hole. There are no clubs to level up, and ties go to whoever finished closer to the pin.

Features

  • Pull. Aim. Fire. Drag back for power, line up the shot, add curl and spin. Wind, bounce and roll decide every approach.
  • Take the turf. Win a hole and it's yours. It pays every hour, shields protect it, and PAYBACK wins it back.
  • Night golf. Courses under city lights and out in deep space, with glowing balls and flame trails.
  • Build and collect. Spend what your turf earns to build planets, and collect golf balls with their own look and flight.
  • Events. HOT TURF windows when every hole pays double, daily gifts and a free pass track.
